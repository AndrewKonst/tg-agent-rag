## iPhone 18 Pro Charging

9to5Mac reported that the iPhone 18 Pro supports faster wired charging than
previous Pro iPhones. The homepage summary said Apple points users to a dynamic
40-watt power adapter with a 60-watt maximum output and a USB-C cable to reach
the maximum charging speed. The summary also described the speed as about 33
percent faster than earlier Pro iPhones.

## AirPods 5

9to5Mac reported that Apple unveiled AirPods 5. The homepage summary described
the new model as starting at $129 and said Active Noise Cancellation is included
as a standard feature.

## Siri AI Timeline

9to5Mac reported that Apple confirmed a Siri AI rollout timeline. The homepage
summary said Siri AI launches in English first and adds five more languages in
October.
