# Remote and Hybrid Work Policy

Owner: People Operations
Document ID: HR-POL-018
Last reviewed: 2026-01-22

## Baseline

Loan Rangers is hybrid by default. Employees attached to an office are
expected on site two days per week, with Wednesday as the shared anchor day
so that every squad overlaps at least once. The second day is agreed within
the squad, not dictated centrally.

Fully remote contracts exist for roles hired as remote. They are not
converted from hybrid contracts on request; if you want to relocate, see
the section on working abroad below.

## Equipment and workspace

The company provides a laptop, a monitor, a keyboard and a mouse for the
home workspace. New joiners have a EUR 500 home-office allowance in their
first year (form HRP-101). You are responsible for a safe workspace and a
connection of at least 50 Mbit/s down and 10 Mbit/s up.

## Security when working remotely

- Connect to the VPN before accessing any internal system.
- Never use public Wi-Fi without the VPN.
- Do not take calls involving Level 3 or Level 4 data in a shared or public
  space. Coffee shops are not meeting rooms.
- Keep the screen lock at ten minutes as enforced by MDM.
- Household members must not use the company laptop, ever.

## Working abroad

You may work from another country for up to twenty working days per
calendar year with manager approval, requested through the People Portal at
least three weeks in advance. Beyond twenty days there are tax and social
security consequences, so it requires approval from People Operations and
Finance, and it may simply not be possible in some jurisdictions.

Working from a country under sanctions or on the restricted list is
prohibited outright, regardless of duration, because of licensing and
information security obligations.

## Core hours and availability

Core hours are 10:00-16:00 in your contracted time zone. Outside those
hours you are not expected to be reachable, and there is no expectation to
answer messages in the evening or at weekends unless you are on call.

Time zone spread within a squad is capped at four hours so that core hours
actually overlap.

## Meetings

Any meeting with one remote participant is a remote meeting: everyone joins
from their own laptop with their own audio. Do not put four people around
one laptop in a room and one person on a screen.
