# Glossary of Terms and Abbreviations

Owner: Knowledge Management
Document ID: KM-GL-001

## Business and regulatory

- **AML** - Anti-Money Laundering. The controls that detect and prevent the
  use of our products to launder criminal proceeds.
- **KYC** - Know Your Customer. The identity verification performed before a
  customer account is activated.
- **PEP** - Politically Exposed Person. Requires enhanced due diligence.
- **SAR** - Suspicious Activity Report. Filed with the regulator by the
  Financial Crime team; never discussed with the customer (tipping off is a
  criminal offence).
- **LTV** - Loan To Value. The loan amount divided by the appraised value of
  the collateral, expressed as a percentage.
- **DSCR** - Debt Service Coverage Ratio. Net operating income divided by
  total debt service. Our minimum for commercial lending is 1.25.
- **APR** - Annual Percentage Rate. The total yearly cost of credit
  including fees.
- **PII** - Personally Identifiable Information. Always Level 4 Restricted
  under ISMS-7.

## Engineering

- **SLO** - Service Level Objective. Our internal reliability target.
- **SLA** - Service Level Agreement. The contractual commitment to a
  customer, always looser than the corresponding SLO.
- **Sev-1** - The highest incident severity: customer-facing outage or data
  loss. Pages the incident commander immediately.
- **RPO / RTO** - Recovery Point Objective (acceptable data loss window) and
  Recovery Time Objective (acceptable restore time).
- **lrctl** - The internal command line tool used for deployments,
  log access and migrations.
- **Ledger** - The double-entry accounting service; the system of record for
  all money movement.

## Internal document prefixes

- **HR-POL / HRP** - People Operations policies and forms.
- **ISMS** - Information Security Management System documents.
- **OPS-RB** - Operations runbooks.
- **FIN** - Finance forms and policies.
- **DRP** - Data Retention Policy documents.
