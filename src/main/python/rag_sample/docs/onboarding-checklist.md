# New Joiner Onboarding Checklist

Owner: People Operations
Document ID: HR-POL-002
Last reviewed: 2026-01-08

## Before day one

People Operations raises ticket `ONB-<employee-id>` in the service desk.
IT prepares the laptop, the Okta account and the hardware security key.
The hiring manager nominates a buddy and books the first-week one-to-ones.

## Day one

- [ ] Collect laptop, badge and FIDO2 security key from the IT desk.
- [ ] Set your Okta password and enrol multi-factor authentication.
- [ ] Install the VPN client and connect once to verify access. The VPN
      profile is distributed via MDM; do not download it from anywhere else.
- [ ] Sign the confidentiality and acceptable-use acknowledgements.
- [ ] Join the mandatory channels: `#announcements`, `#eng-general`,
      your squad channel and `#sec-incident`.
- [ ] Complete security awareness training (required within the first week
      under ISMS-7).

## First week

- [ ] Read the Information Security Policy (ISMS-7) and the glossary.
- [ ] Get read access to the knowledge base and skim the runbooks for your
      area.
- [ ] Pair with your buddy on one real task, however small.
- [ ] Book a coffee with each member of your squad.
- [ ] Submit your equipment preferences via form HRP-101 if you need a
      second monitor, keyboard or standing desk.

## First month

- [ ] Ship one change to production with a reviewer shadowing you.
- [ ] Attend one incident review, even as an observer.
- [ ] Complete AML and KYC awareness training if your role touches customer
      data.
- [ ] Have your 30-day check-in with your manager.

## Probation

The probation period is three months. There is a formal review at the end
of month one and at the end of month three. Feedback is written and shared
with you in advance of the conversation, never sprung on you in the meeting.
