# Expense and Reimbursement Policy

Owner: Finance
Document ID: FIN-88
Last reviewed: 2026-02-05

## How to claim

Submit expenses in the finance portal within thirty days of the spend,
attaching a legible receipt. Claims older than ninety days are not
reimbursed except with written approval from the CFO. Reimbursement is paid
with the next monthly payroll run for claims approved before the 20th.

Card statements are not receipts. A receipt must show the merchant, the
date, the amount and the VAT breakdown where applicable.

## Travel

- Book through the corporate travel agent where possible.
- Economy class for flights under six hours; premium economy is permitted
  above six hours.
- Rail is preferred over air for journeys under four hours.
- Hotels: up to EUR 200 per night in London, Zurich and Dublin; up to
  EUR 140 elsewhere in Europe. Ask Finance before booking above the cap.

## Per diem

A per diem of EUR 55 per full day of travel covers meals and incidentals.
If you claim per diem you may not also claim individual meal receipts for
the same day. Partial days are paid at half rate.

## Equipment and home office

New joiners have a EUR 500 home-office allowance in their first year,
claimed via form HRP-101. Replacement of working equipment is not an
expense claim; raise a ticket in `#it-help` instead.

## What is not reimbursable

- Alcohol beyond a reasonable amount at a client dinner.
- Fines, penalties and parking tickets.
- Personal entertainment, spa and minibar charges.
- Travel upgrades paid for personally and then claimed back.
- Software subscriptions bought without a purchase order. All software
  spend must go through procurement so that a data processing agreement is
  in place; this is an ISMS-7 requirement, not a bureaucratic one.

## Client entertainment

Client entertainment requires pre-approval from your department head and a
note of who attended and why. Anything that could be read as influencing a
regulator, auditor or public official is prohibited outright under the
anti-bribery policy.
